## Category Analysis

- Technology generated the highest sales ($836K) and highest profit ($145K), making it the strongest performing category.
- Office Supplies generated $719K in sales and $122K in profit, showing healthy profitability and consistent business performance.
- Furniture generated strong sales ($742K) but only $18K profit, indicating low profit margins compared to other categories.
- Furniture may require pricing, discount, or cost optimization strategies to improve profitability.


## Region Analysis

- The West region generated the highest sales ($725K) and highest profit ($108K), making it the strongest regional market.
- The East region ranked second with $679K sales and $92K profit, showing strong business performance.
- The South region generated lower sales ($392K) but maintained positive profitability ($47K).
- The Central region generated $501K sales but only $40K profit, indicating potential margin improvement opportunities.
- West and East regions together contribute a significant share of overall business profit and should remain key focus markets.


## Sub-Category Profitability Analysis

- Tables generated the highest loss (-$17.7K), making it the weakest-performing sub-category.
- Bookcases also generated negative profit (-$3.5K), indicating profitability challenges within Furniture products.
- Supplies produced a small loss (-$1.2K) and may require inventory or pricing review.
- Copiers generated the highest profit ($55.6K), making them the most profitable product sub-category.
- Phones ($44.5K) and Accessories ($41.9K) were also major profit contributors.
- The business should investigate why Tables and Bookcases generate losses despite being actively sold.
